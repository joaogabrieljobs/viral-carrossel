# Moont — narrativa visual do carrossel V3

Série fotográfica criada para que os cards usados na captura do Studio formem uma única história visual: produto, fundadora e produção de conteúdo. As três imagens pertencem à mesma marca fictícia brasileira de skincare e repetem, sem rótulos, o mesmo trio de embalagens:

- frasco pump de vidro fosco azul-cobalto;
- pote baixo de vidro chartreuse;
- bisnaga slim vermilion.

Os fundos e props em grafite, a luz dura vinda do alto à esquerda e a textura editorial de médio formato mantêm a continuidade da campanha. A série não contém texto, logotipo, marca d'água ou interface.

## Arquivos finais

- `public/landing/moont-v3/carousel-source/01-product-codes.webp` — 1122 × 1402 px
- `public/landing/moont-v3/carousel-source/02-founder-presence.webp` — 1122 × 1402 px

## Prompts exatos

### 01 — Product codes

```text
Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: premium vertical 4:5 campaign photograph for a Brazilian skincare brand’s Instagram carousel. Primary request: a sculptural still life of one coherent fictitious skincare range, photographed as a real luxury advertising campaign. Scene/backdrop: dark graphite stone tabletop and graphite plaster background, with one restrained matte graphite geometric plinth. Subject: exactly three completely blank, unbranded packages that must define the recurring product family for later scenes: (1) a tall frosted cobalt-blue glass pump bottle with rounded shoulders and a matte cobalt pump, (2) a low circular chartreuse glass cream jar with a flat chartreuse lid, and (3) a slim vermilion-red soft tube standing upright with a small matching cap. Arrange the three products in an elegant asymmetrical sculpture with believable contact shadows and physically plausible scale. Style/medium: sophisticated contemporary British editorial advertising photography, Brazilian color confidence, tactile medium-format film texture, premium but human, no synthetic CGI look. Composition/framing: vertical 4:5, slightly high three-quarter camera angle, generous breathing room, products large enough to read clearly as a family. Lighting/mood: one hard directional studio light from upper left, crisp long shadows, restrained highlights, deep blacks, disciplined art direction. Color palette: cobalt blue, chartreuse, vermilion and graphite only. Materials/textures: frosted glass, translucent glass, soft-touch tube, stone and plaster. Constraints: the packages must be completely blank and carry zero labels or markings; natural perspective; no people; no hands; no extra products; no letters; no words; no numbers; no logos; no branding; no watermark; no UI; no floating objects; no flowers; no bathroom cliché; no gradient glow; no sci-fi styling.
```
