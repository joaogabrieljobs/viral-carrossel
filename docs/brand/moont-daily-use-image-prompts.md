# Moont — imagens de uso cotidiano

Série criada para a landing da Moont com o modo **built-in `image_gen`**. Todas as imagens finais são fotografias 3:2, exportadas em WebP a 1440 × 960 px, com telas escuras e neutras preparadas para a integração da interface real no front-end.

## 01 — Social media no desktop

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: 3:2 landscape lifestyle photograph for a premium SaaS landing page.
Primary request: a Brazilian social media manager actively using a digital content creation platform at a desktop computer during a normal workday.
Scene/backdrop: a contemporary but believable small office or home office in Brazil, clean and lived-in, with only practical desk objects such as keyboard, mouse, water glass and headphones.
Subject: one professional in their late 20s or early 30s, seated naturally, one hand on the mouse and the other near the keyboard, focused on the monitor.
Style/medium: high-end natural editorial photography, understated and credible, sophisticated British digital-product campaign quality without looking like an agency set or a concept shoot; realistic skin, hands, posture and material texture.
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2, medium-wide over-the-shoulder three-quarter view; the computer monitor must be clearly visible, large, mostly front-facing and unobstructed, leaving a clean rectangular screen area suitable for later UI compositing.
Lighting/mood: functional daylight mixed with soft monitor light, calm concentration, restrained contrast.
Color palette: contemporary neutral charcoal, off-white and natural wood with one subtle cobalt detail.
Constraints: monitor displays a plain dark neutral screen with no interface, no words, no icons and no readable logo; person is actively operating the computer; authentic everyday work; no staged pose.
Avoid: holding photos or cards, printed proofs, moodboards, wall collages, abstract props, futuristic holograms, agency-showcase imagery, stock-photo grin, dramatic cinema lighting, warped screen, extra fingers, legible text, brand marks, watermark.
```

Limpeza aplicada à imagem selecionada:

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit
Primary request: remove every written word and typographic mark from the image.
Edits: replace the lettered poster on the left wall with a plain warm plaster wall; remove the words from the wooden crate at right; remove or blur all tiny labels on jars, packets and boxes so no writing remains.
Invariants: preserve the person, pose, face, hands, laptop, blank laptop screen, desk, food products, lighting, camera angle, crop and overall color exactly.
Constraints: the final image contains no readable text, no logos and no watermark.
```
